The demo

Simulated warehouse: the forklift interlock in a closed loop

Four rendered ceiling cameras feed the same vision code the app uses; the simulated forklift obeys Floorsight's slow/stop commands. Results: in-path contacts 2 of 2 without the interlock, 0 of 2 with it (closest 0.70 m and 0.65 m); 27 m driven in 40 s instead of 80 m.
